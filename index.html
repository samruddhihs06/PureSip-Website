About Us

Welcome to PureSip

At PureSip, we believe every sip should be fresh, healthy, and refreshing. We prepare our juices, milkshakes, and healthy specials using carefully selected fruits and quality ingredients. Our goal is to make healthy choices delicious and enjoyable for everyone.

Our Mission

To serve fresh, nutritious, and delicious beverages that inspire a healthier lifestyle every day.

Our Vision

To become the most loved destination for fresh juices and healthy drinks by delivering quality, freshness, and exceptional customer service.

Why PureSip

The PureSip Difference

Fresh fruits selected daily

We source our ingredients straight from regional orchards every single morning, ensuring maximum nutrient density.

Hygienic preparation

We maintain strict sanitation guidelines in our café, sanitizing all items before extraction and preparation.

Healthy and refreshing

No synthetic syrups, colorants, or heavy refined sugars. We offer pure organic hydration that feeds your body.

Affordable prices

High-end wellness should be accessible. We keep our prices transparent and budget-friendly for everyone.

22+ Years experience

Built upon a family heritage of serving high-quality fresh beverages with honesty and care since 2004.

Friendly service

We build lasting relations. We serve you with warm smiles and treat our customers like an extended family.

Our Story

The Roots

PureSip is inspired by a family business that began more than 22 years ago. Watching my father build trust through quality, quantity, honesty, and consistent service inspired me to continue this journey.

The Connection

Many customers still visit our café not only to enjoy freshly prepared juices but also because of the connection they have built with our family over the years.

The Tradition Continues

PureSip continues that tradition by serving every drink with care, freshness, and passion, keeping healthy choices delicious, affordable, and enjoyable for everyone in Hassan.
